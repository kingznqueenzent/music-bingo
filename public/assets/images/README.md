# Kingz & Queenz — image policy

## Never on this site
- AI-generated portraits of people
- Stock models presented as DJ Merci, DJ Liz, staff, or performers
- Any photo a visitor could reasonably think is Merci or Liz until real photos are supplied

## Allowed until real photography arrives
- Official logo / branding
- DJ controllers, mixers, turntables, speakers, microphones
- Stage lighting, dance floors, event venues
- Abstract black / gold / royal-purple atmosphere
- Crowds where individuals are **not** framed as our DJs or staff

## DJ portraits
| Optimized (site) | Raw (archive only) |
|------------------|--------------------|
| `images/dj-merci/` | `raw/dj-merci/` |
| `images/dj-liz/` | `raw/dj-liz/` |

Until `profile.jpg` exists in each optimized folder, UI uses `KingzDjPlaceholder`.
